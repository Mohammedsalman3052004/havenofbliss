Good to know

Frequently asked questions

VBHC Haven of Bliss is a residential project located in Kengeri, Off Mysore Road, South Bangalore. Developed by VBHC Value Homes Private Limited, the project is an 18-acre enclave featuring 19-storey towers. It is a RERA-registered society (PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/040326/008502) offering ready-to-move-in apartments.

The project primarily offers 1 BHK apartments. The 1 BHK units have an approximate area of 540 sq. ft., and are designed for efficient space utilization with modern interiors and picturesque Juliet balconies.

Haven of Bliss offers excellent metro connectivity with two major stations nearby: Challaghatta Metro Station and Kengeri Metro Station. This provides residents with a seamless commute to the Central Business District and other parts of Bangalore.

The project is strategically located near several industrial and tech hubs, including:

  • Global Village Tech Park
  • Bidadi Industrial Estate
  • Major corporations: Bosch Limited, Toyota Kirloskar Motor, and Hindustan Coca-Cola Beverages.

Residents have access to over 15+ ready-to-use amenities designed for fitness and recreation, including:

  • Sports: Cricket Net, Tennis Court, Basketball Court, Sand Volleyball, and Badminton Court.
  • Leisure: Swimming Pool, Clubhouse, Amphitheater, and Central Garden.
  • Convenience: Gym, Children’s Play Area, Jogging Track, Indoor Games, and an on-site Convenience Store.

Yes, the project is surrounded by top-tier social infrastructure:

  • Hospitals: Raja Rajeshwari Medical College & Hospital, BGS Super Speciality Hospital, and SDM Ayurvedic Hospital.
  • Schools/Colleges: National Public School, Delhi Public School, Christ College (Kengeri), and ACS Engineering College.

Yes, VBHC Haven of Bliss offers ready-to-move-in homes. This eliminates construction risk and allows buyers to move in immediately.

VBHC was founded in 2008 by industry veterans Mr. Jaithirth (Jerry) Rao (former Chairman of Mphasis) and Mr. P.S. Jayakumar (former Consumer Banking Head of Citibank and former MD and CEO of Bank of Baroda). The company has a legacy of delivering over 7,200 homes across India.
